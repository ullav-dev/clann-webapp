## Script

---

### HOOK (0:00–0:25)

*[Face-cam. Warm, direct. No intro music yet.]*

Most of us know our grandparents' names.
Maybe our great-grandparents' if we're lucky.

But go back just four generations — your great-great-grandparents —
and most people hit a wall.

Eight people. Eight lives. Eight stories.
And for most of us... complete silence.

That's not a small thing.
Those eight people are *literally why you exist.*

Clann is built to change that.

---

### SERIES INTRO STING (0:25–0:30)

*[Logo animation + music sting: "Welcome to Clann — your family, your story."]*

---

### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN (0:30–0:45)

*[Face-cam. Relaxed.]*

In this first episode, I'm going to show you what Clann is,
what you can do with it,
and why it's different from the big genealogy platforms you might have tried before.

No sign-up required to watch — just come along for the tour.

---

### WHAT IS CLANN (0:45–1:45)

*[Cut to screen recording: Clann landing page]*

Clann is a web app for building and exploring your family tree.

You can run it yourself — it's open source —
or use a hosted version.

*[Navigate to the family members list — show a populated tree with real-looking names]*

At its heart, you have a list of people.
Each person has a name, dates, a photo, and a biography.

*[Click into a person detail page — show the tabs: Family Tree, Relationships, Life Story, Life Events]*

But look what's attached to each person.
A visual family tree. Their relationships.
A life story written in your own words.
A timeline of the events that shaped their life.

*[Click the Life Story tab — show a biography rendered as rich text with a photo]*

This is where Clann gets interesting.
You're not just storing data.
You're writing history.

---

### KEY FEATURES TOUR (1:45–3:00)

*[Screen recording — quick montage, about 15 seconds per feature]*

**The family tree view.**

*[Open FamilyTreeView — show the interactive graph, pan around it]*

This is the tree view. Every person is a node.
Click anyone to jump to their page.
You can flip between vertical and horizontal layouts,
and export the whole thing as an image or a GEDCOM file — the standard format
for sharing family data between apps.

---

**Research workspace.**

*[Navigate to Research page — show the note list and editor]*

This is the research workspace.
Take notes, organise them into folders, search Wikipedia without leaving the app —
and when you find something interesting, save it as a note in one click.

---

**The 1926 Irish Census.**

*[Show the Census search panel]*

If you have Irish ancestry, this one's for you.
The full 1926 Irish Census is now publicly available,
and Clann connects directly to it.
You fill in a name and county — it opens the record right there.

---

**AI Research Assistant.**

*[Open the AI chat panel — show a message being typed]*

And this — this is the one that surprises people.
There's a built-in AI research assistant.
It knows about Irish genealogy, historical records, naming conventions, DNA.
You can ask it anything, and it has context about the person you're looking at.

---

### WHO IT'S FOR (3:00–3:45)

*[Face-cam]*

So who is Clann for?

If you've got Irish ancestry — or you're researching anyone with Irish roots —
Clann has features built specifically for you.

But it works for any family, anywhere.

If you're the person in your family who *cares* about this stuff —
who keeps the old photos, who asks the questions at Christmas —
Clann gives you somewhere to put all of that.

And if you want to share it with family members —
cousins, siblings, grown-up kids — you can invite them in
and give them read access to the whole tree.

---

### RECAP & CTA (3:45–4:30)

*[Face-cam. Warm close.]*

So that's the overview.

In the next episode, we're going to get you set up —
create your account, take a first look around the interface,
and get your very first family tree ready to go.

It takes about five minutes and you'll wonder why you didn't do it sooner.

If this video was useful, hit like — it genuinely helps more people find this series.
Subscribe so you don't miss the next one.

And if you've got questions already — drop them in the comments.
I read every one.

See you in the next one.

*[Outro sting]*
