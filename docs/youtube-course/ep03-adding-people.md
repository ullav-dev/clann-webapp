## Script

---

### HOOK (0:00–0:20)

*[Face-cam. Warm, a little playful.]*

Think of someone in your family.
Someone you knew — or someone you only know through a story.

Now — if that person disappeared from the world tomorrow
and everything about them came only from what you wrote down today —
what would you write?

That's exactly what we're doing in this episode.

---

### SERIES INTRO STING (0:20–0:25)

*[Logo animation + music sting]*

---

### WHAT YOU'LL LEARN (0:25–0:40)

*[Face-cam]*

We're going to add the first real person to your family tree.
Name, dates, biography, photo — everything.
And by the end, you'll have a person card that actually feels like someone.

---

### NAVIGATE TO ADD PERSON (0:40–1:00)

*[Screen recording — the family members page, still empty]*

Here's our family page. Empty. Let's fix that.

*[Click the "+ Add Person" button — the new person form opens]*

Click "Add Person" up here in the top right.

This is the person form. It looks like a lot of fields —
but most of them are optional. We'll fill in the important ones.

---

### NAME FIELDS (1:00–1:45)

*[Screen recording — name section of the form]*

Start with the name.

You've got three name fields: **Given name**, **Middle name**, and **Surname**.
Clann keeps these separate so you can sort and search properly.

*[Type: Given name "Brigid", Surname "Ó Briain"]*

I'm adding Brigid Ó Briain — my fictional great-grandmother.
Feel free to use someone real from your own family.

Below that — **Nickname**.

*[Type: "Bridie"]*

Bridie. A lot of Irish names had an anglicised nickname that's worth recording —
it's often the name that appears in official records,
which matters a lot when you start searching.

---

### SEX FIELD (1:45–2:00)

*[Screen recording — sex field]*

Next — sex.
This isn't just a label. Clann uses this to filter the person picker
when you're adding relationships later.
When you add a Father, you'll only be shown males.
When you add a Mother — females only.
It makes building the tree much faster.

Set Brigid to Female.

---

### DATES (2:00–2:30)

*[Screen recording — birth and death fields]*

Birth and death dates.
Clann is flexible here — you don't need a full date.
"1887" is fine. "circa 1890" is fine.
If you don't know, leave it blank — you can always fill it in later.

*[Type: Date of birth "1887", Date of death "1951"]*

I know Brigid was born in 1887 and died in 1951.
I'll add her place of birth too — Ennistymon, County Clare.

*[Type: Place of birth "Ennistymon, Co. Clare"]*

That location is going to matter when we get to the census episode.

---

### BIOGRAPHY (2:30–3:30)

*[Screen recording — the markdown editor for biography]*

Now — the biography.

This is the part most people skip. Don't skip it.

Even a few sentences now — something you remember being told —
is worth more than perfect data added later.

The editor here supports **markdown** — which sounds technical but isn't.
Two asterisks around a word makes it **bold**.
A hash at the start of a line makes a heading.
A dash makes a bullet point.

*[Type a short biography — something warm and specific]*

> Brigid Ó Briain, known to the family as Bridie, was born in Ennistymon
> in 1887, the second of six children. She was a schoolteacher for over
> thirty years and was known for her fierce opinions about poetry and
> her equally fierce opinions about the weather.
>
> She married Séamus Ó Briain in 1912 and had four children.
> She outlived two of them.

*[Switch to preview mode — show the rendered text]*

Hit preview and it renders as proper formatted text.
On the Life Story tab, this is how it will appear — as a readable,
beautiful piece of writing, not a form field.

---

### PHOTO UPLOAD (3:30–4:00)

*[Screen recording — scroll to the photo section]*

Finally — a photo.

*[Show the photo drop zone]*

Drag and drop any image here, or click to pick one from your files.
JPEG or PNG, up to 2MB.

*[Drag in a period-appropriate portrait photo]*

If you have an old family photo — even a scan of a black and white print —
this is the place for it.

*[Photo appears in the preview circle]*

There she is. Brigid Ó Briain.

Hit Save.

---

### SAVE & VIEW (4:00–4:15)

*[Screen recording — person detail page opens]*

And here's her card.

Name, dates, biography.
There are four tabs here — Family Tree, Relationships, Life Story, and Life Events.
We're going to explore all of these in the coming episodes.

For now — she exists. She's in the tree.
That's the first step.

---

### TIPS & GOTCHAS (skip inline — woven into demo above for this episode)*

*[Face-cam — brief]*

One thing worth saying out loud:

You are never going to have complete information.
You will never know all the dates, all the spellings, all the places.

That's not a problem. That's genealogy.

Clann is designed for uncertainty.
Add what you know. Leave notes about what you don't.
The gaps are part of the story too.

---

### RECAP & CTA (4:15–4:45)

*[Face-cam]*

So — you've added your first person.
Name with proper name fields, a nickname that might save you later,
dates you know, a biography that'll mean something in twenty years,
and a photo that makes it real.

In the next episode, we're going to connect people.
Brigid needs a husband, some children, maybe a sibling or two.
And I'll show you how Clann makes that much faster than you'd expect.

If this was useful, give it a like — it helps others find the series.
Subscribe, drop questions in the comments.

See you in the next one.

*[Outro sting]*
