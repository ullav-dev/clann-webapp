### TIPS & GOTCHAS (3:10–3:55)

*[Face-cam + brief return to screen as needed]*

A couple of things that trip people up at this stage.

**First:** the confirmation email. Sometimes it lands in spam — especially with corporate email addresses. If you don't see it in your inbox, check spam before hitting "resend".

**Second:** your tree name and your tree's display name are slightly different.
The name is a short identifier — no spaces, no special characters.
The display name is what you see in the app — that can be anything you like,
including fada characters if you're working with Irish names.

*[Back to screen — show the tree in the selector]*

So "obriain-family" might be the tree name, and "Ó Briain Family" is the display name.
Don't worry too much about the technical name — Clann handles it.

**Third:** Clann automatically sets your first tree as your **primary** tree.
That's the one you'll land on each time you log in.
You can change which tree is primary later — but for now, whatever you created first is it.
